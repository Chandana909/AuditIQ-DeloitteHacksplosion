Problem Definition
Challenges
Fragmented Financial Data - Invoices, POs, GRNs,
and approval trails
High ly repetitive audit processes involving
multiple large data sources.
Variation in policies between organizations that
impacts approval validation
Lack of explainable prioritization of high-risk
exceptions
Core Problem
The problem is not lack of financial data, but lack of audit-ready intelligence across connected financial evidence.Context
Large amounts of invoices, purchase records,
goods receipts, tax documents, and approval
trails are handled by organizations
The auditor has to validate transactions by
checking for duplicate transactions, GSTIN
compliance, and internal approval policies
The methods currently used are slow,
repetitive, and lack priority on actual high-risk
transactions
Solution Approach  
How the Solution Works?
 POC Build:  Financial records (ERP / invoices / PO / GRN /
approvals) are ingested and normalized before audit
analysis.
Financial masking  is performed on the data before agent
execution for secure enterprise deployment
Controls-First Design: Approval logic, vendor rules, tax
controls (FASB / GST controls etc.) are executed before
AI reasoning.
Governed Outcomes:  High-risk anomalies are sent
through Human -in-the-Loop review, while all the
decision have a machine-verifiable audit trail.
Client-Configurable Policy Engine : Approval logic,
vendor rules, tax controls can be customized to client
requirements.The financial evidence is unified  under a
transaction layer that is audit-ready across invoices,
PO/GRN, vendor records, and approval records.  
Deterministic audit controls execute first using
three-way matching, compliance checks, and
client-defined rules.  
The multi-agent intelligence  checks for timing
anomalies, vendor collusion indicators, invoice
splitting, and vendor relationships before
determining a severity score.  
High-risk exceptio ns are routed to Human-in-the-
Loop review , and auditors can also leverage Realm
AI to query and investigate flagged transactions and
associated evidence.Design Principles
Unique Differentiators  
Forensic-Grade Traceability (ISA 230-Aligned Design): Machine-verifiable chain of evidence: rule → evidence → agent →
reviewer → outcome
Three-Way Matching at Full Population Scale : All PO-GRN-Invoice rows are matched, eliminating the sample space where
unknown exceptions persist.
Proactive Tax Intelligence: Unlike traditional reactive audits, the multi-agent system detects missed tax credits and
recommends structural changes to the procurement process to achieve maximum tax efficiency.
Multi-Agent Architecture : Specialisation Over Generalisation: Seven agents verify financial risk stepwise rather than
forcing monolithic models to generalise across all possible cases.
Policy Engine Built for Every Client : Customise approval logic, vendor rules, and compliance rules without modifying the
audit workflow.
AI-Assisted Vendor Intelligence: Cross-checks GST taxpayer profiles with external data to detect collusion rings and
unknown vendor risks outside the scope of internal ERP data.
Human-in-the-Loop Explainable Audit Governance (Approve/Reject/Override with justification)
GenW modules:
AppMaker - financial data ingestion, workflow orchestration, and audit UI layer
Agent Builder - multi-agent audit engine with configurable Human-in-the-Loop decisions
Playground - anomaly dashboards, risk views, and audit analytics
Realm AI - conversational audit copilot for evidence exploration

Architectural Diagram
What will the user see?
Interactive Analytics Dashboard: An interface using the GenW Playground platform that shows the visualization of
risks using heat maps and duplicate clusters and displays the results of the internal compliance checks.
Vendor Intelligence View: This interface will reveal the presence of shell companies that have high risks using the risk
metrics calculated through autonomous external lookups and GST lookups.
Explainable AI Reasoning Interface: Using the GenW Realm AI assistant interface, the user can get the "Why?" for
the risks using the "Why Flagged?" interface and access the actual cited sources for the evidence in the form of
actual financial data sources.
Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Review Queue: This interface will allow the auditor to review the escalations that have
been flagged by the agents and allow the auditor to approve or reject the decisions while recording the reasons for
the decision.
Automated Audit Packs: This interface will contain the structured and automatically generated output in the form
of exception reports and links to the actual sources and the entire audit log.

Scalability Potential
Data & Pipeline Scalability: Concurrently processes
thousands of rows without breaking. It leverages high-
throughput REST APIs and distributed processing to normalize
everything from Excel ledgers to complex ERP data.
Architectural & Token Scalability: The Multi-Agent
Architecture  acts as decoupled microservices. Layered
financial validation through specialised agents  allows
seamless addition of new rules while drastically reducing LLM
token consumption and API costs.
Functional Extensibility: The platform natively supports multi-
department and multi-entity audit monitoring, with the
flexibility to extend into continuous auditing and complex
forensic investigation workflows over time.

Scalability & Beneficiaries
Beneficiaries
A. Efficiency & Coverage
Parallel Execution: 5 deterministic control agents execute in parallel, which
drastically reduces the audit time.
100% Population Coverage: Analyzes the entire population, not just a
sample, to uncover hidden fraud.
B. Risk Detection & Prioritization
Mathematical Risk Scoring: Offers a weighted formula to provide a strict
ranked queue of risks based on data, not just an opinion provided by the AI.
External Context: Adds real-time GST and internet feeds to identify unknown
risks outside the organization's data.
C. Governance & Compliance
Compliant Evidence Chain: Offers complete lineage from the rule to the
reviewer to satisfy ISA 230 and NIST AI RMF.
Tamper-Evident Logging: Requires justifications for Human-in-the-Loop
changes to be written back into the log to close the loop.
